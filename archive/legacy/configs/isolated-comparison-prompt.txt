SHARED FACTS (not a prescribed storyboard)
For real b,c and f(x)=x^2+b*x+c, completing the square gives
f(x)=(x+b/2)^2+c-b^2/4.
The vertex is (-b/2,c-b^2/4). Define Delta=b^2-4*c, so vertex height=-Delta/4.
Delta>0 gives two distinct real roots; Delta=0 gives one distinct real root (a double root); Delta<0 gives no real roots. "No real roots" does not mean "no roots over all number systems."
Your lesson must meaningfully cover all three cases and explain the connection between vertex height, x-axis intersections and Delta. It must not imply that changing b has no effect, or that the vertex is always on the y-axis.
